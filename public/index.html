    How trust works

    1. Organisers vouch for each other

      A few known London organisers start the network. To post a venue you need a vouch. If someone you vouched for posts junk, you lose the right to vouch.

    2. Every field shows its source

      Capacity, AV, evening access and terms each record who added them and when they were last confirmed. Venues come from events that actually happened there, not from a map.

    3. Contacts stay private

      Free rooms are a shared resource, so venue contacts are members-only. The assistant calls or emails the venue for you, and every answer is saved so one call serves every organiser.
